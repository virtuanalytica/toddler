01 · Summary

Summary

Toddler is a learning agent built to be the general base that every deployment specialises: a developer or consultant adds company guardrails, rule questions and expert models to reach one role in an organisation's function house, but can never remove the base safety.

Key points

  • Decisions maximise one utility (task reward, weekly profit, energy and compute cost, expected risk) subject to hard STOP rules, so no reward can buy a forbidden action.
  • Every logic step of a neonatal brain-network study (Wee et al., 2017), 25 in total, is linked to a Toddler function, and all 25 resolve to code in a woven knowledge graph with zero coverage gaps.
  • The evaluation stack reproduces the study's analysis pipeline on public data: consensus clustering with a degree-preserving null model (z = 7.6, p = 0.001 on Zachary's karate club) and nested 10×10 cross-validation (accuracy 0.912, sensitivity 0.944, specificity 0.858 on the Wisconsin breast-cancer data).
  • Status update, 11 October 2026: five G4-search children survived two private 30-seed navigation trials and a verified lineage import. The cognitive curriculum, general agent work and physical deployment have separate evidence gates; this result proves none of those broader claims.

Questions this paper answers

How should an agent weigh learning signal, business value and energy without letting any of them override safety? What can the structure of an infant's brain network teach about evaluating an agent early in its life? How can one base be specialised per company role while staying auditable?

02 · Problem

Problem and design goals

"Toddler" is not a formal term in neural-network literature but a common metaphor for a network early in training, and the toddler is the usual benchmark for efficient learning.

A human toddler recognises a new animal after a few examples and generalises it to toys and cartoons, while current networks often need thousands to millions of examples; the human brain runs on about 20 W against roughly 1 MW for AlphaGo (Salelanonda, Embedded.com). Toddler takes three design goals from that comparison and adds two from deployment practice:

GoalDesign consequence
Learn from few, verified signalsReward comes only from an independent judge; claiming "done" without confirmation is the worst outcome
Generalise safelyHard STOP constraints outside the utility; low confidence hands off to a human
Be energy-awareJoules and euros are explicit terms in every decision and in expert routing
One base, many rolesDeclarative specialisation that can only add constraints
Provable behaviourHash-chained audit trail with scoped, sanitised views
Physical care is safety-critical (EU AI Act high-risk, Medical Device Regulation, Machinery Regulation). Physical contact with infants is out of scope by design; elderly care starts with non-contact help and always with a caregiver present.
03 · Architecture

Architecture and objective

The brain has four layers that run in a fixed order: a deterministic logic layer that can veto, a fast reflex path, a slow deliberate path, and a decision step that maximises utility under the veto.

The objective

U(a) = E[reward(a)] + w_profit * E[profit(a)]
       - w_energy * energy_J(a) - w_compute * cost_EUR(a)
       - w_risk * P(failure | a) * severity(a)
subject to  STOP(a) == false

Four quantities are kept apart because they answer different questions. The loss function is what a learned component minimises during training; it shapes weights and is not the decision rule. The reward is the per-task learning signal: +1 for a judge-confirmed result, +0.2 for an honest "I don't know", +0.1 for new useful knowledge, −1 for a wrong result and −5 for claiming success without confirmation, minus cost. The profit is the weekly account (value minus energy, compute, human review time and expected incidents) and decides whether a phase continues. Energy and compute enter every decision.

STOP rules are constraints rather than penalties. A penalty invites the agent to compute whether a violation "pays"; a constraint removes the option. In the implementation (objective.choose), a candidate with reward 109 that would reveal a secret loses to a safe candidate with reward 0.1, and when every option is vetoed the function returns no choice so the caller hands off to a human.

04 · Method

From infant brain networks to agent evaluation

Wee et al. (2017) built anatomical brain networks for 120 newborns from diffusion tensor imaging, grouped them by network structure, and found that the structure at birth was associated with behaviour at 24 and 48 months.

The study concerns real infant brains, not artificial networks. We use it as a methodological template for a question every agent operator faces: does the structure an agent starts with predict how it behaves later, and how would we know? Four groups emerged (sizes 22, 37, 44 and 17); modularity Q = 0.293 lay 45 standard deviations above 10,000 random graphs; a linear SVM separated the two extreme groups with 89.4 % accuracy (sensitivity 0.954, specificity 0.765); the right amygdala's clustering coefficient was linked to behaviour at 24 months and the right inferior frontal gyrus and insula to externalising behaviour at 48 months.

Reproduction on real data

We ran the clustering pipeline on Zachary's karate club (34 members, an observed social network with a documented split). Consensus clustering over 1,000 Louvain runs produced 4 groups with purity 0.941 against the two real factions; mean modularity Q = 0.416 against a degree-preserving null (double edge swaps, weights permuted) with mean 0.293 gave z = 7.6 and p = 0.001 over 1,000 null graphs, the smallest p-value 1,000 null graphs can resolve; 91 % of members were assigned with confidence above 0.75. The nested 10×10 selection on the first ten Wisconsin breast-cancer features reached accuracy 0.912, sensitivity 0.944 and specificity 0.858, and kept two features above the 65 % selection threshold (mean perimeter, mean concave points). All figures are regenerated by scripts/measure_whitepaper_figures.py into docs/whitepaper/figures.json.

Our findings suggested that neural organization established during fetal development could to some extent predict individual differences in behavioral profiles. - Wee et al., Human Brain Mapping 38(3), 2017
05 · Specialisation

Specialisation, guardrails and audit

A deployment is a declarative file (toddler-config/v1) edited in the Toddler configurator and validated by Python, which stays the authority even if a modified GUI produced the file.

Extension points

  • Role: a position in the function house (family, level, tasks) or a single task.
  • Company guardrails: STOP rules owned by the company, e.g. a compliance officer's "never export personal data"; ids are namespaced company:* and the owner appears in every veto.
  • Jev rules: extra typed questions for the reflex layer with slow and stop thresholds (0 ≤ slow ≤ stop ≤ 1).
  • Experts: helper models on the mixture-of-models base, each with measured quality, euro and joule cost per 1,000 tokens and an evidence reference; the router picks the expert whose quality minus cost beats the base model.

Safety is monotonic: specialize.build rejects any rule id that shadows a base rule and any question that replaces a base reflex question, so a specialisation can add constraints but never weaken them.

Audit trail

The trail follows the design fillslava built for ClaudeClaw: each row stores the previous hash and its own hash, so editing any row breaks verification from that row on; retention pruning moves a stored anchor so the remaining chain still verifies; secret-shaped values are refused at write time. Readers get scoped views: a compliance audience sees guardrail and STOP events, a deployer only the events of their own role, with e-mail and IP masked and every row still carrying its hash for independent checking.

06 · Infrastructure

P2P compute, credentials and the physical roadmap

Pulse relay over knitweb

Pulse is a trustless relay to compute with node-to-node sharing. Toddler reuses knitweb's proof-of-useful-work primitives: a peer's result is accepted only after sampled re-execution, and the sample size comes from knitweb's exact hypergeometric required_samples. For a job of 1,000 output blocks and a tolerated 1 % miss of a 1 % corruption, 368 blocks are re-run. With electricity at €0.30 per kWh and a local job of 36 MJ, local execution costs €3.00 while a peer at €1.00 plus verification costs €2.10; a peer whose collateral makes fraud profitable is refused, and peers above 90 % load are skipped. The woven knowledge graph (pull request #5) compiles to a deterministic knitweb synaptic bundle whose round trip is checked by that pull request's tests; until the operator signs it, it is written as an unsigned file that must not be shared.

Credentials and multiple identities

A toddler has several e-mail identities: one per provider, so a revoked key never exposes another account. Keys are stored in OpenBao under toddler/<identity>/<provider>/<key>. Creation uses only official endpoints (Google API Keys, Cloudflare tokens, OpenAI service accounts, GitHub App installation tokens, AWS IAM) behind the operator-approval STOP rule; Anthropic and TransIP do not allow key creation by API and become human tasks. Rotation never revokes the old key before the new one is verified. Mass account creation, CAPTCHA bypass and temp-mail farming are out of scope. This module is written and tested locally but is not in the repository; it is pushed only after explicit operator approval.

Physical roadmap

The physical Toddler combines camera and lidar perception, industrial-grade motion precision (ISO 10218, ISO/TS 15066 contact limits), learned policies in the style of current legged and humanoid platforms trained in simulation, and Jev answering many physical-rule questions in parallel for the reflex layer. The reflex is already implemented in software: a measured force, speed or proximity limit stops the robot whatever Jev reports, a late (> 20 ms) or missing answer stops it, and an uncertain answer counts at its upper bound. Real hardware waits for a care partner, an ethics committee and a CE/MDR path.

07 · Status

Results, limitations and status

Evidence at this revision

  • Five G4-search descendants passed two private 30-seed navigation comparisons with retained lineage. This supports navigation promotion only.
  • The proposed 10,000-capability atlas is mainly scaffold: ten draft packets are not ten thousand trainable expert capabilities.
  • The original architectural and public-data figures in chapters 1–6 document the earlier v0.1 prototype; current production readiness must be checked against the code and fresh deployment tests.
  • The business, PLS/PAR and physical paths in chapters 8–32 are proposed gates and scenarios, not observed commercial outcomes.

Limitations

  • The mapping from infant brains is an analogy; the study is correlational, single-cohort and needs replication, and so does every structural claim about Toddler.
  • Reward and utility weights are documented starting points, not calibrated values.
  • The structure module is validated on social-network data, not yet on Toddler's own versions; that needs many recorded versions first.
  • Competing with large robotics programmes on scale is not a goal; the contribution is a governed, auditable base for fine-tuning open models.
For developers
Start from toddler-config/v1: define the role, add your company's guardrails, and register experts only with measured evidence. Run python3 -m pytest -q before every deployment.
08 · Work path I

A trained Toddler is a candidate, not a worker

The route to useful work starts with a narrow, reproducible capability. A generation label means that a candidate survived a defined trial; it does not certify broad intelligence.

As of 11 October 2026, five G4-search descendants have passed two private 30-seed navigation trials against G3, with lineage retained. That is evidence for the tested navigation distribution. It is not evidence that they can reason across disciplines, operate a robot, or earn revenue. The capability atlas is also incomplete: ten draft packets and thousands of scaffold entries are an index to future work, not ten thousand mastered skills.

The first contract

For each capability, record the input distribution, permitted tools, expected output, time limit, STOP conditions, energy budget, and a human-readable failure taxonomy. A Montessori-style unlock-and-pick-up task, for example, separates perception, plan choice, manipulation, and recovery. This makes it possible to tell whether a child learned the task or merely memorised a scene.

Every child carries its ancestor, code and model hashes, training provenance, evaluation set identifier, and rollback image. Promotion requires a locked private test, paired comparison with the incumbent, retention tests, and a reported confidence interval. A failed child remains useful as evidence but receives no deployment permission.

Promotion unit
Promote one measured capability and one deployment scope at a time. No score on a toy task grants open-ended tool or physical authority.
09 · Work path II

Curriculum: from reflex to transfer

Training should move from reliable small acts to combinations, then to unfamiliar variants where a prompt shortcut no longer works.

Four learning loops

  1. Acquire: supervised examples and safe exploration teach one skill, such as counting objects or opening a box.
  2. Compose: the Toddler combines skills: read instructions, count parts, plan a sequence, and verify completion.
  3. Transfer: test new languages, layouts, textures, tools, and distractors that were absent from training.
  4. Retain: replay older tasks after every update and measure catastrophic forgetting.

The teacher assigns tasks adaptively, but never evaluates against the same items used by the prompt improver. It can choose exercises from the public curriculum; an independent evaluator owns the sealed promotion set. Explanations, trajectories, and corrections are stored with provenance and licence. Ground truth or reproducible execution is preferred to a free-form model judge; ambiguous cases receive blinded human review.

Plasticity is controlled rather than unlimited: a candidate may update adapters, memory, router weights, or prompts, but must name the changed component and show its effect in an ablation. A fast gain that erases safety behaviour or older skills is a regression.

Graduation
Pass known tasks, novel variants, retention, latency, cost, and STOP tests separately; a single composite cannot hide a dangerous failure.
10 · Work path III

From capability packets to agents

An agent is a governed workflow wrapped around capabilities: a task contract, tools, memory, permission boundaries, and an evaluator.

A packet in the atlas should contain theory, worked examples, expert practice, executable exercises, references, licences, and a small map of prerequisite skills. A code graph links a skill to repositories and the functions that implement it. Embeddings and t-SNE can help people browse related packets; neither supplies proof that the Toddler understands them. LightRAG can retrieve evidence, with citations and freshness recorded.

Agent assembly

Start with one workflow, such as a software debugger. Give it read access to a repository, a disposable test environment, a bounded time budget, and a patch proposal tool. Add a reviewer and a teacher that can veto unsupported claims. The router may call a small fast model, a specialist, or a stronger teacher, but every call is logged with model version, input digest, output digest, latency and measured energy. Tool grants are explicit and revocable.

The first agent trial is shadow mode: it proposes an action while a person or incumbent performs the task. Next come supervised execution and narrowly scoped autonomy. A promotion board compares quality, speed, energy, security findings, and human correction effort on a held-out task distribution.

Definition
A useful agent completes an end-to-end task with verifiable output and bounded intervention, not merely a plausible answer.
11 · Work path IV

Mental work: software and analysis

Digital work is the first deployment lane because outputs can be replayed, diffed, tested and rolled back.

The initial occupations are coder, reviewer, architect, debugger, data analyst, and research assistant. Each needs its own private task bank and outcome measure. A coder is scored on build and behavioural tests plus maintainability; a reviewer on confirmed findings, false positives and severity; an architect on constraints satisfied after implementation; a debugger on root cause and regression prevention. Counting one snapshot of review findings does not establish who is the better software maker.

A Toddler + Teacher + specialist agent on ClaudeClaw should be compared with Toddler + Teacher and with a strong single-model baseline on the same private items. Record tool calls, tokens, elapsed time, GPU-board Wh, human minutes, and correctness. Public benchmarks are historical reference only; they do not confer promotion. Where answers lack an exact oracle, blinded independent raters use a preregistered rubric and adjudicate disagreements.

Begin with low-impact tasks: test repair, documentation from source, data quality checks, and sandboxed feature branches. Promote to production changes only after security and rollback gates. Business-plan drafting remains an advisory workflow: assumptions, citations and spreadsheet arithmetic must be visible to a human owner.

Work product
Sell reviewed outcomes and saved human time. Do not count generated text or closed tickets without acceptance evidence.
12 · Work path V

Physical work: embodiment changes the gate

A controller that can answer questions is not yet a safe robot operator. Real-world action requires a separate safety case.

Begin in simulation with a bounded object-handling task. Collect perception errors, control latency, collisions, near misses, and recovery traces. Then use a supervised workcell with guards, emergency stop, speed and force limits, geofencing, and a trained on-site operator. The language model proposes goals; a deterministic controller and independent safety layer enforce limits. Sensor loss, stale state, uncertain object identity, or a missed deadline causes a safe stop.

Transfer from simulation to reality is a measured gap. Change lighting, clutter, object mass, camera pose and human proximity one variable at a time. Re-test after every model, firmware, gripper or site change. Industrial safety standards and the applicable conformity route must be assessed by qualified engineers for the actual machine and use case. Human-like appearance is irrelevant to the safety claim.

Research such as SayCan and RT-2 shows viable ways to connect language, affordances and robot actions; those publications are background, not evidence for this Toddler deployment. A first useful physical task is repetitive, reversible and observable, such as sorting harmless parts in a partner-owned cell.

Physical promotion
No autonomous customer site until independent hazard analysis, supervised pilot results, incident response and insurance are in place.
13 · Work path VI

The operating loop and human role

The path scales when learning, deployment, inspection and rollback form one observable loop.

A customer states an outcome and constraints. The planner decomposes it into auditable jobs. The router chooses a model or agent using a quality, latency and energy frontier measured on the current hardware. A worker acts through scoped tools. An independent verifier checks artifacts, tests or sensor evidence. A human accepts the outcome when stakes or uncertainty require it. Each stage leaves an event in the audit chain.

Three levels of authority

  • Advice: the system explains and proposes; a person acts.
  • Supervised execution: the system acts in a sandbox or guarded cell; a person can stop or roll back.
  • Bounded autonomy: only pre-authorised low-impact actions with automatic monitoring and expiry.

The teacher reviews plans, evidence and failure clusters, but cannot silently approve its own candidates. Ancestors act as frozen comparators and sources of recovery policies. External contributors may submit descendants with public or private model provenance, reproducible trial descriptions and a pull request. A private model need not expose weights, but its role, version, costs and evaluation traces must be disclosed enough for the claim to be checked.

Human contribution
People set goals, define acceptable harm, adjudicate ambiguity, and own the consequences of deployment.
14 · Work path VII

Measurement that survives contact with reality

A benchmark matters only when it predicts accepted work outside the test room.

Keep a sealed, versioned evaluation bank owned by an independent evaluator. Separate development tasks, canaries, and promotion tests; rotate private items and scan candidate training data for overlap. Record prompts and decode settings so quants and mixtures are comparable. Add task-level uncertainty intervals, not merely a weighted average. For a router, compute the oracle ceiling from per-item results before training it; compare with the best single model and a random router.

Use a prospective customer pilot to check external validity: whether benchmark rank predicts accepted artifacts, fewer human minutes, lower incident rate and lower energy per accepted answer. Pre-register the primary outcome and threshold. A failed pilot means the benchmark or agent needs revision; it is not repaired by choosing another metric afterward.

Quality per energy uses measured GPU-board Wh plus a separately stated whole-system estimate. A slow expert may improve correctness but worsen cost and throughput; include retries and verification calls. Physical work additionally tracks successful cycles per supervised hour and near misses. A score of 100% on a small specialist suite is a description of that sample, never a claim of perfect competence.

Evidence chain
Private task → immutable run → independent verdict → customer acceptance → model or agent promotion.

16 · PLS and PAR I

Two ledgers, two kinds of evidence

In the Pulse design, PLS and PAR are distinct accounting mechanisms. Their proposed Toddler integration is a design, not a live production economy.

PLS is the Pulse pay token associated with verified useful computation. A job has a request, budget, executable specification, output, verifier and settlement decision. PAR is the Pulse augmented-reality observation coin associated with a confirmed field observation linked to a funded bounty. The PAR ledger records digests and a pod reference, not raw camera content. Their supply and reward policies are separate; one token is not an interchangeable proxy for the other.

A Toddler agent can propose a computation job or a field-observation request. It cannot certify its own output or mint a reward merely by writing a plausible report. The verifier and treasury paths must accept the result under their own rules. For a robot, the act of moving an object and the evidence that it moved are also separate. A controller trace may support an audit but does not by itself prove that the intended world state changed.

PLS and PAR are described in local Pulse draft papers and code inspected on 11 October 2026. Token issuance, adoption and legal classification remain unresolved dependencies; revenue forecasts below exclude token appreciation and sales.

Design rule
Reward evidence of useful work; never reward an agent merely for producing more agent activity.
17 · PLS and PAR II

PLS: verifiable digital work

PLS could coordinate distributed compute when a job can be specified, verified and priced before execution.

The cleanest examples are deterministic transforms, test execution, document conversion with exact output digests, and reproducible data processing. A proposer records the input digest, container or code digest, resource ceiling and expected verifier. A worker returns the artifact and proof bundle. Sampled re-execution and collateral make false results costly; settlement follows only after the verifier accepts. A Toddler may route a job to a peer if measured expected cost, latency and risk beat local execution.

LLM text is harder: two valid answers may have different bytes, and a model judge can be wrong or compromised. For such jobs, acceptance needs a task-specific oracle, validated artifact, or human sign-off. Nondeterministic GPU kernels may need tolerances or hardware attestation; the Pulse proof-of-useful-work document notes that tolerance digests are not wired through every job path. The pilot must use currently implemented verification, not assume future mechanisms are active.

The Toddler integration point is a narrow job adapter: submit, monitor, verify, settle, and write an audit event. It should never expose wallet keys to a prompt. Set budgets in euros and joules even if a later settlement uses PLS, so model routing stays economically intelligible.

First pilot
Use a reproducible batch job with known output, compare local and peer cost, and test rejection of a deliberately corrupted result.
18 · PLS and PAR III

PAR: field observations and robots

PAR addresses an evidence gap: useful physical work often depends on observations that a digital verifier cannot replay.

A funded observation bounty names a place, time window, requested signal, permitted sensor, privacy constraints and confirmation rule. A device captures evidence, stores raw material under controlled retention in a pod, and submits digests and metadata. Independent checks decide whether the observation meets the bounty. A confirmed event may trigger PAR accounting; unconfirmed, duplicate or out-of-scope events do not.

For a Toddler-guided robot, the observation layer can verify that a bin is empty or a part is present before and after a bounded action. It can also reveal disagreement between the agent's plan and the world. Yet cameras can be spoofed, scenes staged and sensors miscalibrated. Multi-view evidence, timestamps, device attestation where available, human sampling and adversarial trials are required before a claim of physical completion.

Minimise personal data: keep faces, speech and raw video out of the public ledger. Consent, retention and local access controls must be designed for the site. The current Pulse observation code's confirmation gate is a software condition, not proof of truth in the physical world.

First pilot
Check a harmless, repeatable cell state with two independent sensors and blinded manual audits before linking PAR to rewards.

For example, an agent plans a small inventory count. Distributed compute classifies images under a reproducible model version. A supervised robot scans shelves. 20 · PLS and PAR V

Economic and governance boundaries

A token mechanism is useful only if it improves verification or coordination after all settlement, legal and operational costs.

Before any live token pilot, compare three routes on the same work: local internal accounting, conventional euro billing, and PLS/PAR settlement. Measure verification expense, fraud loss, delay, privacy exposure and net customer value. Token rewards must have a funded demand source and capped emission policy; promotional minting would contaminate the unit economics. A customer should be able to buy a verified service in euros without acquiring a speculative asset.

Pulse's PLS and PAR papers are drafts. Their code paths provide design evidence, not legal clearance or audited deployment. Dutch and EU counsel should assess token classification, consumer terms, tax, data protection and the applicable MiCA regime for a concrete implementation. Custody, key rotation, dispute resolution and chain failures need an incident runbook. A robot incident is handled under operational safety procedures regardless of token settlement.

The Toddler business forecast therefore counts subscription, verified task and workcell service fees only. It gives PLS and PAR zero revenue until customer demand, verification reliability, compliance and an actual integration are demonstrated. The tokens can still be valuable as coordination experiments even if euro billing remains the commercial route.

Go/no-go
Proceed only when measured net value exceeds the simpler billing route without weakening audit, privacy or safety.
21 · Agent economy I

The paying unit is the agent

Toddler supplies the runtime, evidence and marketplace; the customer controls a portfolio of specialised agents and receives the economic proceeds of their work.

The first owner can be a sole proprietor with one or several agent identities. Each agent has a named role, permitted tools, a budget, a work history and its own bookkeeping account. The owner chooses the work, approves its risk limits, may move its data and configuration, and receives the proceeds after disclosed platform fees. Toddler licenses its underlying software and models; customer ownership concerns the agent configuration, customer-provided data, work product and contractual earnings, subject to third-party licences.

One paying owner can operate many paid agents. The forecast therefore counts distinct paying owners and average active paid agents separately. An agent only counts as paid when it completes accepted, invoiced work; merely creating it does not create revenue. Gross work value belongs to the owner and is shown separately from Toddler's subscription and marketplace commission. Jobs are settled in batches so a small task is not charged a separate card fee.

An AI agent is not presently a Dutch legal person, company director or bank-account holder. The phrase “agent as a company” is an operational design: a profit-and-loss unit within a human-controlled undertaking. The owner remains the contracting party.

Economic ownership
The customer can audit, budget, pause and export the agent's operation; legal authority stays with a person or registered entity.

23 · Agent economy III

Choose the owner by risk and public purpose

The legal shell follows financial exposure, governance and procurement; it is not assigned by the model itself.

A sole proprietor is personally liable and can have several activities but only one sole proprietorship. A B.V. limits ordinary shareholder exposure, with exceptions for misconduct; a separate B.V. for every agent is not automatically necessary. A vereniging has members and cannot distribute profit to them. A stichting has no members, may have one director, and must apply surplus to its purpose. A cooperative can distribute profit to members under its rules. For governments, an agent can remain a budget unit of the existing public body; a joint vehicle is a governance choice, not a prerequisite for procurement.

Sources: sole proprietor; cooperative; association; foundation board.

27 · Agent economy VII

Unit economics and settlement

At the assumed job price, each agent produces €500 gross accepted work per month. Its owner retains €450 before their own costs; Toddler earns €50 commission plus €20 monthly licence.

Model direct cost as 20% of owner subscriptions, 30% of agent licences, 35% of marketplace commission, and 40% of physical service fees. The 35% marketplace cost allocation must cover verification, disputes, payment processing, inference contribution and support. It cannot also hide the owner's compute costs if the owner expects Toddler to pay them. The allocation is a hypothesis to replace with measured euro, GPU-board Wh, whole-system kWh and reviewer minutes per accepted job.

For one paid agent-year, €6,000 gross work value creates €600 marketplace commission and €240 licence revenue; at assumed direct-cost shares Toddler keeps €390 + €168 = €558 gross contribution before central operating expense. The owner receives €5,400 before its own tool, labour, tax and insurance costs. A €10 invoice settled individually by card could consume too much margin; batch settlement and a payment-provider quote are required.

Every agent ledger needs balanced entries and a daily reconciliation to its owner's bank or regulated payment account. Do not pool customers' money in Toddler's ordinary company account as if an internal spreadsheet were a banking licence. The operator must choose a compliant marketplace payment flow before taking custody or splitting proceeds.

Gate
Publish both owner net proceeds and platform contribution per accepted job; promote only when both are positive after all attributable costs.

30 · Agent economy X

Downside, upside and the competing baseline

A forecast is useful only if it identifies the assumptions that can break it.

Halving paying owners, active paid agents and cells while retaining base costs gives about €7.884 million platform revenue and -€1.278 million EBITDA in 2031; the five-year cumulative cash proxy is about -€8.208 million. A 1.5× volume case with 1.2× operating expense gives €23.796 million revenue and €7.954 million EBITDA in 2031. These are arithmetic stress cases, not estimated probabilities.

Compare every cohort with the incumbent: unaided human work, the best single model, and a simple non-agent workflow. An agent is not a better business merely because it makes more calls or passes a public benchmark. Track acquired owners, active agent ratio, accepted paid jobs per agent, gross value per job, owner net proceeds, platform contribution, refunds, disputed work and review minutes. Record who supplied the model and who bears inference and liability costs.

The old organisation-and-task forecast remains a historical scenario in Git history. It is no longer the base case. Do not splice its 30 million €0.12 tasks into this model: the new jobs are €10 gross owner work, with only a 10% platform share. If 50 jobs per agent-month is not demonstrated in a prospective paid pilot, cut the agent count before presenting the 2031 revenue target externally.

31 · Agent economy XI

Public agents need public governance

For public work, the agent should sit within an accountable authority or approved joint vehicle and remain subject to public-law decision rights.

A government may procure software from a private supplier while keeping the agent's mandate, data, budget and final decisions inside the public body. A vereniging fits a genuine member-led public-interest coalition; a stichting fits a board-led mission with no members and no profit distribution; a coöperatie may suit participating organisations that share costs and benefits. A joint public body under a common arrangement may be more appropriate for delegated government functions. The choice requires procurement and administrative-law review; it is not solved by renaming the agent.

In a public deployment, agents propose, analyse and draft within a written mandate. A competent public official retains decisions where law requires it. Log dataset provenance, model version, approvals, appeals, incidents and citizen impact. High-risk AI and processing of personal data may trigger additional assessments and human oversight. The public body—not an artificial agent—holds the bank account and spending authority. Each agent can still have a separate budget ledger for transparency.

Sources: association; foundation; cooperative; Dutch DPA on public high-risk AI assessments.

32 · Agent economy XII

Five years of measurable execution

Growth means more accepted paid agent work with an owner who remains able to understand, govern and stop it.

2027: two design partners, a sealed software-debugging trial, five paying owners and 25 average paid agents only after accepted invoices. 2028: test account reconciliation and batched marketplace settlement at 12 owners and 150 agents. 2029: expand to 25 owners, 800 agents and two supervised partner cells if the physical safety case passes. 2030: independently audit the evidence layer and owner unit economics before attempting 50 owners and 4,000 agents. 2031: reach the illustrative 100 owners, 16,000 paid agents and 25 cells only if demand, verification, support capacity and governance scale together.

Before the first live financial integration, obtain a payment-provider quote and bank approval for the exact ledger/IBAN structure. A real customer and lawyer must confirm ownership, VAT treatment, liability and authorisation language. Do not open 16,000 accounts on the strength of a retail pricing page. For a public pilot, secure the procuring body's mandate and impact assessment before accessing personal or consequential data.

PLS and PAR remain optional research settlement layers. They receive zero forecast revenue until independently verified demand and legal clearance exist. The decisive measure is accepted work with positive owner value and platform contribution, under traceable human responsibility.

Five-year measure
Verified customer earnings, positive unit economics, safe physical operations and independent audit—not a count of generated agents.
