08 · Work path I

A trained Toddler is a candidate, not a worker

The route to useful work starts with a narrow, reproducible capability. A generation label means that a candidate survived a defined trial; it does not certify broad intelligence.

As of 11 October 2026, five G4-search descendants have passed two private 30-seed navigation trials against G3, with lineage retained. That is evidence for the tested navigation distribution. It is not evidence that they can reason across disciplines, operate a robot, or earn revenue. The capability atlas is also incomplete: ten draft packets and thousands of scaffold entries are an index to future work, not ten thousand mastered skills.

The first contract

For each capability, record the input distribution, permitted tools, expected output, time limit, STOP conditions, energy budget, and a human-readable failure taxonomy. A Montessori-style unlock-and-pick-up task, for example, separates perception, plan choice, manipulation, and recovery. This makes it possible to tell whether a child learned the task or merely memorised a scene.

Every child carries its ancestor, code and model hashes, training provenance, evaluation set identifier, and rollback image. Promotion requires a locked private test, paired comparison with the incumbent, retention tests, and a reported confidence interval. A failed child remains useful as evidence but receives no deployment permission.

Promotion unit
Promote one measured capability and one deployment scope at a time. No score on a toy task grants open-ended tool or physical authority.
09 · Work path II

Curriculum: from reflex to transfer

Training should move from reliable small acts to combinations, then to unfamiliar variants where a prompt shortcut no longer works.

Four learning loops

  1. Acquire: supervised examples and safe exploration teach one skill, such as counting objects or opening a box.
  2. Compose: the Toddler combines skills: read instructions, count parts, plan a sequence, and verify completion.
  3. Transfer: test new languages, layouts, textures, tools, and distractors that were absent from training.
  4. Retain: replay older tasks after every update and measure catastrophic forgetting.

The teacher assigns tasks adaptively, but never evaluates against the same items used by the prompt improver. It can choose exercises from the public curriculum; an independent evaluator owns the sealed promotion set. Explanations, trajectories, and corrections are stored with provenance and licence. Ground truth or reproducible execution is preferred to a free-form model judge; ambiguous cases receive blinded human review.

Plasticity is controlled rather than unlimited: a candidate may update adapters, memory, router weights, or prompts, but must name the changed component and show its effect in an ablation. A fast gain that erases safety behaviour or older skills is a regression.

Graduation
Pass known tasks, novel variants, retention, latency, cost, and STOP tests separately; a single composite cannot hide a dangerous failure.
10 · Work path III

From capability packets to agents

An agent is a governed workflow wrapped around capabilities: a task contract, tools, memory, permission boundaries, and an evaluator.

A packet in the atlas should contain theory, worked examples, expert practice, executable exercises, references, licences, and a small map of prerequisite skills. A code graph links a skill to repositories and the functions that implement it. Embeddings and t-SNE can help people browse related packets; neither supplies proof that the Toddler understands them. LightRAG can retrieve evidence, with citations and freshness recorded.

Agent assembly

Start with one workflow, such as a software debugger. Give it read access to a repository, a disposable test environment, a bounded time budget, and a patch proposal tool. Add a reviewer and a teacher that can veto unsupported claims. The router may call a small fast model, a specialist, or a stronger teacher, but every call is logged with model version, input digest, output digest, latency and measured energy. Tool grants are explicit and revocable.

The first agent trial is shadow mode: it proposes an action while a person or incumbent performs the task. Next come supervised execution and narrowly scoped autonomy. A promotion board compares quality, speed, energy, security findings, and human correction effort on a held-out task distribution.

Definition
A useful agent completes an end-to-end task with verifiable output and bounded intervention, not merely a plausible answer.
11 · Work path IV

Mental work: software and analysis

Digital work is the first deployment lane because outputs can be replayed, diffed, tested and rolled back.

The initial occupations are coder, reviewer, architect, debugger, data analyst, and research assistant. Each needs its own private task bank and outcome measure. A coder is scored on build and behavioural tests plus maintainability; a reviewer on confirmed findings, false positives and severity; an architect on constraints satisfied after implementation; a debugger on root cause and regression prevention. Counting one snapshot of review findings does not establish who is the better software maker.

A Toddler + Teacher + specialist agent on ClaudeClaw should be compared with Toddler + Teacher and with a strong single-model baseline on the same private items. Record tool calls, tokens, elapsed time, GPU-board Wh, human minutes, and correctness. Public benchmarks are historical reference only; they do not confer promotion. Where answers lack an exact oracle, blinded independent raters use a preregistered rubric and adjudicate disagreements.

Begin with low-impact tasks: test repair, documentation from source, data quality checks, and sandboxed feature branches. Promote to production changes only after security and rollback gates. Business-plan drafting remains an advisory workflow: assumptions, citations and spreadsheet arithmetic must be visible to a human owner.

Work product
Sell reviewed outcomes and saved human time. Do not count generated text or closed tickets without acceptance evidence.
12 · Work path V

Physical work: embodiment changes the gate

A controller that can answer questions is not yet a safe robot operator. Real-world action requires a separate safety case.

Begin in simulation with a bounded object-handling task. Collect perception errors, control latency, collisions, near misses, and recovery traces. Then use a supervised workcell with guards, emergency stop, speed and force limits, geofencing, and a trained on-site operator. The language model proposes goals; a deterministic controller and independent safety layer enforce limits. Sensor loss, stale state, uncertain object identity, or a missed deadline causes a safe stop.

Transfer from simulation to reality is a measured gap. Change lighting, clutter, object mass, camera pose and human proximity one variable at a time. Re-test after every model, firmware, gripper or site change. Industrial safety standards and the applicable conformity route must be assessed by qualified engineers for the actual machine and use case. Human-like appearance is irrelevant to the safety claim.

Research such as SayCan and RT-2 shows viable ways to connect language, affordances and robot actions; those publications are background, not evidence for this Toddler deployment. A first useful physical task is repetitive, reversible and observable, such as sorting harmless parts in a partner-owned cell.

Physical promotion
No autonomous customer site until independent hazard analysis, supervised pilot results, incident response and insurance are in place.
13 · Work path VI

The operating loop and human role

The path scales when learning, deployment, inspection and rollback form one observable loop.

A customer states an outcome and constraints. The planner decomposes it into auditable jobs. The router chooses a model or agent using a quality, latency and energy frontier measured on the current hardware. A worker acts through scoped tools. An independent verifier checks artifacts, tests or sensor evidence. A human accepts the outcome when stakes or uncertainty require it. Each stage leaves an event in the audit chain.

Three levels of authority

  • Advice: the system explains and proposes; a person acts.
  • Supervised execution: the system acts in a sandbox or guarded cell; a person can stop or roll back.
  • Bounded autonomy: only pre-authorised low-impact actions with automatic monitoring and expiry.

The teacher reviews plans, evidence and failure clusters, but cannot silently approve its own candidates. Ancestors act as frozen comparators and sources of recovery policies. External contributors may submit descendants with public or private model provenance, reproducible trial descriptions and a pull request. A private model need not expose weights, but its role, version, costs and evaluation traces must be disclosed enough for the claim to be checked.

Human contribution
People set goals, define acceptable harm, adjudicate ambiguity, and own the consequences of deployment.
14 · Work path VII

Measurement that survives contact with reality

A benchmark matters only when it predicts accepted work outside the test room.

Keep a sealed, versioned evaluation bank owned by an independent evaluator. Separate development tasks, canaries, and promotion tests; rotate private items and scan candidate training data for overlap. Record prompts and decode settings so quants and mixtures are comparable. Add task-level uncertainty intervals, not merely a weighted average. For a router, compute the oracle ceiling from per-item results before training it; compare with the best single model and a random router.

Use a prospective customer pilot to check external validity: whether benchmark rank predicts accepted artifacts, fewer human minutes, lower incident rate and lower energy per accepted answer. Pre-register the primary outcome and threshold. A failed pilot means the benchmark or agent needs revision; it is not repaired by choosing another metric afterward.

Quality per energy uses measured GPU-board Wh plus a separately stated whole-system estimate. A slow expert may improve correctness but worsen cost and throughput; include retries and verification calls. Physical work additionally tracks successful cycles per supervised hour and near misses. A score of 100% on a small specialist suite is a description of that sample, never a claim of perfect competence.

Evidence chain
Private task → immutable run → independent verdict → customer acceptance → model or agent promotion.

16 · PLS and PAR I

Two ledgers, two kinds of evidence

In the Pulse design, PLS and PAR are distinct accounting mechanisms. Their proposed Toddler integration is a design, not a live production economy.

PLS is the Pulse pay token associated with verified useful computation. A job has a request, budget, executable specification, output, verifier and settlement decision. PAR is the Pulse augmented-reality observation coin associated with a confirmed field observation linked to a funded bounty. The PAR ledger records digests and a pod reference, not raw camera content. Their supply and reward policies are separate; one token is not an interchangeable proxy for the other.

A Toddler agent can propose a computation job or a field-observation request. It cannot certify its own output or mint a reward merely by writing a plausible report. The verifier and treasury paths must accept the result under their own rules. For a robot, the act of moving an object and the evidence that it moved are also separate. A controller trace may support an audit but does not by itself prove that the intended world state changed.

PLS and PAR are described in local Pulse draft papers and code inspected on 11 October 2026. Token issuance, adoption and legal classification remain unresolved dependencies; revenue forecasts below exclude token appreciation and sales.

Design rule
Reward evidence of useful work; never reward an agent merely for producing more agent activity.
17 · PLS and PAR II

PLS: verifiable digital work

PLS could coordinate distributed compute when a job can be specified, verified and priced before execution.

The cleanest examples are deterministic transforms, test execution, document conversion with exact output digests, and reproducible data processing. A proposer records the input digest, container or code digest, resource ceiling and expected verifier. A worker returns the artifact and proof bundle. Sampled re-execution and collateral make false results costly; settlement follows only after the verifier accepts. A Toddler may route a job to a peer if measured expected cost, latency and risk beat local execution.

LLM text is harder: two valid answers may have different bytes, and a model judge can be wrong or compromised. For such jobs, acceptance needs a task-specific oracle, validated artifact, or human sign-off. Nondeterministic GPU kernels may need tolerances or hardware attestation; the Pulse proof-of-useful-work document notes that tolerance digests are not wired through every job path. The pilot must use currently implemented verification, not assume future mechanisms are active.

The Toddler integration point is a narrow job adapter: submit, monitor, verify, settle, and write an audit event. It should never expose wallet keys to a prompt. Set budgets in euros and joules even if a later settlement uses PLS, so model routing stays economically intelligible.

First pilot
Use a reproducible batch job with known output, compare local and peer cost, and test rejection of a deliberately corrupted result.
18 · PLS and PAR III

PAR: field observations and robots

PAR addresses an evidence gap: useful physical work often depends on observations that a digital verifier cannot replay.

A funded observation bounty names a place, time window, requested signal, permitted sensor, privacy constraints and confirmation rule. A device captures evidence, stores raw material under controlled retention in a pod, and submits digests and metadata. Independent checks decide whether the observation meets the bounty. A confirmed event may trigger PAR accounting; unconfirmed, duplicate or out-of-scope events do not.

For a Toddler-guided robot, the observation layer can verify that a bin is empty or a part is present before and after a bounded action. It can also reveal disagreement between the agent's plan and the world. Yet cameras can be spoofed, scenes staged and sensors miscalibrated. Multi-view evidence, timestamps, device attestation where available, human sampling and adversarial trials are required before a claim of physical completion.

Minimise personal data: keep faces, speech and raw video out of the public ledger. Consent, retention and local access controls must be designed for the site. The current Pulse observation code's confirmation gate is a software condition, not proof of truth in the physical world.

First pilot
Check a harmless, repeatable cell state with two independent sensors and blinded manual audits before linking PAR to rewards.
19 · PLS and PAR IV

Joining the digital and physical loops

A useful robot job can combine PLS-backed computation with PAR-backed observation, while keeping their proofs distinct.

For example, an agent plans a small inventory count. Distributed compute classifies images under a reproducible model version. A supervised robot scans shelves. Separate observations verify location and stock count. The customer accepts the inventory file after discrepancy review. PLS could settle the compute job; PAR could recognise confirmed observations. Neither token should bypass the customer acceptance gate.

Use a common event ID and cryptographic references to join records, while access control keeps private material off public infrastructure. Reconciliation must tolerate reorganisation, delayed evidence and disputed observations. If a verifier is unavailable, the work pauses or follows a documented human exception; it does not silently mark itself complete.

Integration test
Trace one job end to end, then inject a false compute result and a false sensor report; both must be rejected independently.
20 · PLS and PAR V

Economic and governance boundaries

A token mechanism is useful only if it improves verification or coordination after all settlement, legal and operational costs.

Before any live token pilot, compare three routes on the same work: local internal accounting, conventional euro billing, and PLS/PAR settlement. Measure verification expense, fraud loss, delay, privacy exposure and net customer value. Token rewards must have a funded demand source and capped emission policy; promotional minting would contaminate the unit economics. A customer should be able to buy a verified service in euros without acquiring a speculative asset.

Pulse's PLS and PAR papers are drafts. Their code paths provide design evidence, not legal clearance or audited deployment. Dutch and EU counsel should assess token classification, consumer terms, tax, data protection and the applicable MiCA regime for a concrete implementation. Custody, key rotation, dispute resolution and chain failures need an incident runbook. A robot incident is handled under operational safety procedures regardless of token settlement.

The Toddler business forecast therefore counts subscription, verified task and workcell service fees only. It gives PLS and PAR zero revenue until customer demand, verification reliability, compliance and an actual integration are demonstrated. The tokens can still be valuable as coordination experiments even if euro billing remains the commercial route.

Go/no-go
Proceed only when measured net value exceeds the simpler billing route without weakening audit, privacy or safety.
21 · Business concept I

Sell dependable work, not a human illusion

The proposed company is a privacy-first work platform built around Toddler, Teacher and specialist agents. Its commercial promise is a bounded result with an audit trail.

The entry customer is an organisation that already has a backlog of repetitive software, data-quality or research tasks and cannot send all inputs to an external API. Its pain is not a shortage of generated prose; it is the cost of reviewing, reproducing and securing outcomes. The first sale is an annual orchestration subscription. Additional independently verified tasks are metered. Physical work is added as a software-and-service fee for customer- or partner-owned cells only after a supervised safety pilot.

The world may develop much more capable agents and robots during these five years. The base forecast does not assume that Toddler reaches human parity. It requires a series of narrow workflows that save measurable customer time. Robot-market adoption is context, not an addressable-market multiplier. IFR reported 542,076 industrial robot installations worldwide in 2024; that number does not imply demand for this product.

Customers buy in euros. PLS and PAR may later coordinate verifiable compute and observations, but speculative token value is excluded from the plan. The scarce asset is a trusted dataset of task attempts, corrections, tests and accepted work, governed by customer consent and provenance.

Customer promise
A result that can be inspected, repeated or reversed, with clear human accountability.

23 · Business concept III

Go to market through narrow pilots

Start with workflows that have objective acceptance, frequent repetition, and a clear budget owner.

Year one targets eight paying organisations, reached through two design-partner pilots and a controlled expansion to software teams, data operations and privacy-sensitive research groups. Every pilot has a baseline period, a shadow period, and a decision gate. The buyer sees accepted-task rate, human review minutes, incident count, turnaround time and fully allocated cost. Reference cases must disclose the task distribution and denominator.

Distribution can combine direct technical sales, open-source contributions, and system integrators. Crowdsourced Toddler descendants are welcome through pull requests carrying lineage and trial evidence, but commercial deployments use a reviewed release channel. A partner may run its own private model; the contract still requires the version, licence constraints and evaluation method to be stated. The platform does not promise that a contributor's private data becomes public.

Physical expansion begins with a robot integrator and a single guarded site. Industrial and service robotics are already active markets, yet each cell needs site-specific economics and engineering sign-off. Do not count a workcell as active until it completes a paid supervised month and meets the safety and customer acceptance gate.

Sales discipline
Convert a pilot only when the customer can name the accepted outcome, its previous cost, and the person accountable for the new process.

25 · Business concept V

Unit economics before scale

The plan is viable only if accepted-work margins survive retries, verification and customer support.

Model direct delivery cost as 25% of workbench revenue, 40% of verified-task fees, and 35% of cell-service fees. These shares include inference, GPU electricity, verification, storage and direct service labour. They are hypotheses. Meter whole-system kWh per accepted task and distinguish it from GPU-board Wh; a night queue does not make incremental power free. For reference, Eurostat reported an EU non-household average electricity price of €0.1837/kWh for the second half of 2025; a specific site may differ materially.

At €0.12 per verified task, a 40% direct-cost share leaves €0.072 gross contribution. Ten million accepted, separately billable tasks would therefore contribute €720,000 before operating expense. If an average task needs repeated expensive teacher calls, the 40% assumption fails. The gate is not token throughput but net contribution per accepted result, measured prospectively.

At €24,000 per customer-year, a 25% direct-cost share leaves €18,000 before sales, research and administration. At €72,000 per active cell-year, a 35% direct-cost share leaves €46,800. Cells require a separate allowance for field support and incident response; a partner owns the machine and ordinary hardware depreciation under this model.

Unit gate
Do not scale a workflow with negative gross contribution after retries and verification, even when its benchmark score is excellent.

28 · Business concept VIII

Downside, upside and falsification

A forecast should tell us what would disprove the plan soon enough to change it.

The downside case halves paying organisations, billable tasks and active cells while retaining base operating and capital spending. In 2031 it yields €8.160m revenue and -€2.902m EBITDA; its five-year cumulative cash proxy is -€12.259m. The upside case scales those volumes by 1.5 and operating expense by 1.2, retaining capital spending; 2031 revenue becomes €24.480m and EBITDA €6.594m. These are arithmetic sensitivities, not likelihood estimates.

Test the most uncertain assumptions first: customer conversion, the share of tasks independently accepted, human review minutes, direct cost per retry, and the time needed to certify a physical cell. One quarter of weak conversions should trigger pricing and product interviews, not a bigger sales target. If a teacher improves quality but uses too much energy or reviewer time, route it only to tasks where the gain pays for itself.

A live dashboard should compare actuals with this model every month. Show forecast error by assumption, not just total revenue. Keep published benchmark results, work acceptance and sales claims separate. Early pilots may invalidate the 2031 scale and still reveal a valuable smaller company.

Decision threshold
After two paid pilots, revise the model from measured conversion, acceptance and cost. If no repeatable positive gross contribution exists, narrow or stop the offer.
29 · Business concept IX

Five years of responsible execution

The age of more capable agents and robots increases the value of human choice, verifiable work and safe deployment.

2027: prove one digital workflow with sealed tests and eight paying organisations. 2028: add two supervised physical cells only if the safety case passes. 2029: standardise the evidence layer and partner integration across seventy organisations. 2030: test whether wider routing and specialist portfolios improve accepted-work economics at 160 organisations. 2031: reach sustained service quality at the modelled 320 organisations and seventy cells, or publish why the path was revised.

Workers and customers retain decision rights. Deployments should document what tasks are automated, which new supervisory work appears, how errors are appealed, and who may stop the system. A more human-like conversation interface should never be confused with consciousness, competence or legal personhood. The company should invest in training operators and reviewers as deliberately as in models.

PAR and PLS can be tested as optional evidence and coordination mechanisms after operational and legal gates. A robot partnership should be allowed to use ordinary euro invoices and independent logs. The company succeeds if it delivers accepted outcomes with accountable human governance, whether or not agents ever equal people broadly.

Five-year measure
Repeatable customer acceptance, positive unit economics, safe physical operations and independently auditable claims.
