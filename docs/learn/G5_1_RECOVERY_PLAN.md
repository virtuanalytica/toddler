# G5.1 recovery plan after the first prospective hold

Status 11 October 2026: the semantic G5 candidate is retained as an
unpromoted research artifact. Its two-family prospective gate returned
`failed_hold_no_promotion`: one fresh confirmation family was below the
predeclared cognitive floor, although matched navigation was retained in both
families. The failed private banks are spent and are never training data.

## What the result permits us to say

The semantic expert improves some arithmetic transfer cases, but the result is
not stable enough across independent families to claim a promoted generation.
The test covered English and Dutch arithmetic only. It says nothing about
Cantonese comprehension, the other cognitive areas, software work, physical
control or a general-purpose G5.

The public aggregate can guide curriculum design. It cannot reveal private
questions, answer keys or seed values. Any analysis of the held-out result must
remain at aggregate cell level and must not be used to select examples or
hyperparameters for the current candidate.

## G5.1 curriculum changes

1. **Separate language and operation competence.** Build a public development
   curriculum with independently reviewed English and Dutch templates for
   addition, subtraction and multiplication. Balance every language-operation
   cell and include narrative, comparative and direct forms.
2. **Review Dutch wording before training.** A native reviewer signs the public
   answer-key bundle and records ambiguities, number formatting and naturalness.
   A second reviewer resolves disagreements without seeing any future holdout.
3. **Teach compositional arithmetic.** Add public examples where the operation
   cue changes while the operands and answer distribution remain matched. Keep
   operand values disjoint from all prior public lessons and every spent private
   family.
4. **Keep the fallback exact.** The G4 cognitive base and all five navigation
   parents remain bytewise frozen. A new expert may answer only its registered
   arithmetic scope and must fall back to G4 on uncertainty.
5. **Compare ablations.** Before a new candidate is frozen, compare the
   unchanged G4 base, the current semantic expert and the G5.1 expert on a new
   public development/check split. Record the selected architecture and failed
   recipes; do not tune on a private result.

## New promotion attempt

The evaluator must create a new owner-only bank family after the G5.1
curriculum and native-language review are committed. It must use new IDs,
questions, operands and seed commitments, and exclude every Teacher lesson,
both earlier transfer banks and both failed confirmation banks. The same
conjunctive thresholds remain in force: each confirmation needs at least
44/72 G5 correct, at least 12 net paired gains, at most two G4-only losses, no
negative language-operation cell, an exact one-sided paired p-value below
0.005 and exact navigation retention for every measured seed.

Two successful confirmation families are required. A single successful family
cannot rescue a failed one. The evaluator writes only a prompt-free aggregate
to Git and never appends a `survived` lineage event until the independent
semantic review, source import and broader capability gates are complete.

## Exit criteria for G5.1

- public curriculum and Dutch review are hash-pinned;
- public development selection is complete before private-bank preparation;
- both fresh confirmation families pass every threshold;
- navigation, non-arithmetic fallback and parent hashes are unchanged;
- no private prompt, seed, answer or failed-bank row enters training;
- the scope is still reported as arithmetic until separate language,
  Montessori, software and physical-control gates pass.
