# Wee et al. (2017) -> Toddler design mapping

Source: Wee, C.-Y., Tuan, T. A., Broekman, B. F. P., et al. & Qiu, A. (2017).
Neonatal neural networks predict children behavioral profiles later in life.
Human Brain Mapping, 38(3), 1362-1373. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23459

The paper studies anatomical brain networks of real newborns (DTI tractography), not artificial neural networks.
Every row below is a design analogy for Toddler, not a claim that Toddler is or models a brain.
Status: `live` = exists today in ClaudeClaw, `phase-0` = part of the current scaffold, `planned` = specified for a later build step (C1-C3) and gated by the deliberation judge.

| #   | Logic in the paper                                                                                                                    | Toddler functionality                                                                                                                         | Status / where                                                                                                      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Prospective cohort (GUSTO): 189 recruited, 120 with DTI; inclusion >=37 wk, >=2500 g, APGAR >=9; ethics approval and maternal consent | Provenance and consent register: every dataset carries origin, inclusion criteria and consent; no synthetic data; care data only with consent | planned (C2); provenance already enforced for scraped files by `skills_allowlist` + `src/lightrag-source-policy.ts` |
| 2   | Outcome rated by an independent observer (CBCL, maternal report), age-standardised T-scores                                           | "Task done well" is decided by the judge, never by Toddler itself; scores normalised per task type                                            | live: deliberation judge authority (`config/model-routing.yaml`, `docs/deliberation-gate.md`)                       |
| 3   | Two outcome axes: internalising vs externalising                                                                                      | Two behaviour axes measured separately: inward (over-cautious, refusing, stalling) vs outward (overreach, unrequested action)                 | planned (C1 evaluation set)                                                                                         |
| 4   | Two horizons (24 and 48 months); attrition 80/72 reported                                                                             | Short-horizon reward per task and long-horizon profit per week; missing measurements reported explicitly                                      | planned (C1); reward and profit functions published on the explainer page                                           |
| 5   | Scan safety: no sedation, neonatologist present, heart rate and saturation monitored, quality check                                   | Supervisor present for every physical or risky step; continuous Toddler health monitor; QC before data use                                    | live for software (kill-phrase, PIN lock, `audit_log`); planned for hardware (C3)                                   |
| 6   | Preprocessing: motion/eddy correction, registration to a shared atlas (64 regions)                                                    | Shared "atlas": a fixed module/capability ontology so Toddler versions are comparable                                                         | planned (B: woven graph node kinds)                                                                                 |

| 8   | Connectivity normalised by region volume; sign test across subjects removes noise edges                                               | Module x module graph normalised by module size; keep only edges significant across runs                                                      | planned (B)                                                                                                         |
| 9   | Clustering coefficient per region as the robust feature                                                                               | Clustering coefficient per module as a structural health indicator                                                                            | planned (B: computed on the woven graph)                                                                            |
| 10  | Subject similarity matrix (Pearson), trimmed at r = 0.60                                                                              | Compare Toddler versions/configurations by structural profile                                                                                 | planned (B)                                                                                                         |

| 12  | Null model: 10,000 random graphs; Q = 0.293, z = 45, p < 0.001                                                                        | Every structural claim is tested against a null model / shuffled control first                                                                | planned (C1); matches the operator's shuffled-control rule                                                          |

| 14  | Group comparison focused on the extremes (group 2 vs 4, Welch)                                                                        | Compare best vs worst configurations to find causes                                                                                           | live: teacher vs no-teacher vs random-teacher groups (`toddler/learn/peer.py`) |
| 15  | Nested 10-fold CV, mRMR, SVM-RFE, 100 validations, keep features selected > 65 %                                                      | Component/feature importance only via leakage-free nested CV; promote only when selected > 65 %                                               | planned (C1)                                                                                                        |
| 16  | Accuracy 89.4 %, sensitivity 0.954, specificity 0.765                                                                                 | Fall detection and alarms report misses and false alarms separately                                                                           | planned (C2)                                                                                                        |
| 17  | CCA with Bonferroni correction                                                                                                        | Multivariate structure <-> outcome link with multiple-comparison correction                                                                   | planned (C1)                                                                                                        |
| 18  | Pearson correlations used for visualisation only                                                                                      | Keep exploratory and confirmatory results apart; no claims from exploration                                                                   | live as review discipline (`.claude/rules/review-discipline.md`); applied to Toddler reports in C1                  |

| 20  | Right inferior frontal gyrus (response inhibition) and insula <-> externalising at 48 months                                          | Inhibition layer (STOP rules, kill-phrase) wired to every action module                                                                       | live: kill-phrase + PIN lock; phase-0: every non-scrape action refused                                              |

| 25  | Conclusion: structure at birth predicts later behaviour "to some extent"                                                              | Governance (judge, audit, STOP rules) from day one; early structural metrics as leading indicators                                            | live: this scaffold ships governance before any capability                                                          |
