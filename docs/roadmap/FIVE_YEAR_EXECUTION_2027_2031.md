The 100-pair minimum is an operational floor, not guaranteed statistical power. If the interval remains wide, collect more sealed tasks without changing the registered threshold. A scorecard with missing whole-system energy reports missingness; it cannot turn absent readings into zero cost. Public development tasks are rejected by the scorer.

## Annual decision gates

| Period | Target activity | Decision needed before widening scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | One accepted digital role; target 5 paying owners and 25 average active paid agents | Repeatable accepted invoices and positive owner and platform contribution |
| 2028 | Standardise agent ledgers and batched settlement; target 12 owners and 150 active paid agents | Reconciled owner accounts, bounded payment authority and no unresolved critical disputes |
| 2029 | Scale evidence API; target 25 owners, 800 active paid agents and 2 supervised cells | Independent claim audit, site safety and real paid demand per agent |
| 2030 | Expand specialist portfolio; target 50 owners, 4,000 active paid agents and 8 cells | Prospective work economics better than incumbent workflows, with proven support capacity |
| 2031 | Scenario target 100 owners, 16,000 average active paid agents and 25 partner cells | Sustainable margins, independent governance and revised forecast from actual cohorts |

Every target is conditional. The new base case reaches €15.840m **platform** revenue and €3.988m EBITDA in 2031, but needs a €4.300m cumulative EBITDA-minus-capex trough through 2030 before working capital and taxes. A 25% buffer gives a preliminary €5.375m envelope, not a financing ask. The 16,000 agents imply €96m of *owner-side* accepted work value at 50 jobs of €10 each per agent-month; Toddler recognises only a 10% marketplace commission plus its licence and other fees. The downside scenario remains loss-making in 2031. No token price, token sales, agent bank balances or owned robot fleet are counted as platform revenue.

## Workstreams and responsibilities

1. **Research and Teacher:** keep G4 navigation lineage frozen; build independent cognitive and retention trials before any broader promotion; document curriculum and model provenance.
2. **Agent engineering:** start with the debugger; integrate scoped tools, reproducible sandboxes, rollback, and the evidence schema. Keep Teacher reviews distinct from independent outcome verification.
3. **Evaluation:** own sealed tasks and customer acceptance rubrics; monitor contamination, inter-rater agreement, energy coverage and prospective validity.
4. **Commercial operations:** interview design partners, test €6,000 per owner-year, €240 per active agent-year, a 10% commission on accepted work and cell fees; replace annual targets with monthly signed contracts, active paid cohorts and collection data.
5. **Physical engineering:** partner-owned guarded cell only; hazard analysis and supervised observation precede bounded autonomy.
6. **Pulse research:** compare ordinary euro billing with PLS compute settlement and PAR field observation on controlled pilots. No live token claim until technical, privacy and legal gates pass.
7. **Finance and legal:** maintain one immutable internal ledger per agent, reconcile to the human or legal entity's real bank account, obtain provider approval before provisioning agent-specific IBANs, and validate marketplace settlement with payments counsel. See [agent ownership and banking](AGENT_OWNERSHIP_AND_BANKING.md).
8. **Public-sector governance:** let the public body own the agent budget and mandate; evaluate a member-led association, mission-led foundation, cooperative or joint public body only when the governance and procurement case demands it. A Dutch foundation can have one director; three entities controlled by the same person do not create independent oversight.

## Monthly review packet

Publish a dated internal packet containing: paying owners and churn; active paid agents and time-to-first-paid-job; accepted jobs and owner-side gross value by role; owner net proceeds and platform contribution after payment, inference and review costs; independent-verifier coverage; review minutes, elapsed time, GPU-board Wh and whole-system kWh per accepted job; disputed work, critical incidents and near misses; bank-ledger reconciliation; forecast-versus-actual variance; open rollback and privacy issues. Keep customer material private and publish only aggregate claims with denominators and methods.

The first packet cannot be filled from benchmark scores. It waits for prospective work records. The scorecard's evidence hash and the audit trail should be stored with each packet so later revisions can be traced.
