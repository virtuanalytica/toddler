The 100-pair minimum is an operational floor, not guaranteed statistical power. If the interval remains wide, collect more sealed tasks without changing the registered threshold. A scorecard with missing whole-system energy reports missingness; it cannot turn absent readings into zero cost. Public development tasks are rejected by the scorer.

## Annual decision gates

| Period | Target activity | Decision needed before widening scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | One accepted digital role, then a second; target eight paying organisations, 0.2m separately billable verified tasks | Repeatable paid acceptance and positive gross contribution after retries and review |
| 2028 | Standardise agent service and supervised physical pilot; target 25 organisations, two partner-owned cells | Site-specific safety case, operator training, insurance, zero unresolved critical incidents |
| 2029 | Scale evidence API and partner integration; target 70 organisations, eight active cells | Independent audit of claim quality and site-to-site reproducibility |
| 2030 | Expand specialist portfolio and routing; target 160 organisations, 25 cells | Prospective customer outcome and cost frontier better than incumbent workflows |
| 2031 | Mature digital and physical service; scenario target 320 organisations, 30m verified tasks, 70 cells | Sustainable margins, independent safety governance and a revised forecast based on actuals |

Every target is conditional. The forecast's base case reaches €16.320m revenue and €2.696m EBITDA in 2031, but needs a €4.320m cumulative EBITDA-minus-capex trough through 2030 before working capital and taxes. A 25% buffer gives a preliminary €5.40m envelope, not a financing ask. The downside scenario remains loss-making in 2031. No token price, token sales or owned robot fleet are counted.

## Workstreams and responsibilities

1. **Research and Teacher:** keep G4 navigation lineage frozen; build independent cognitive and retention trials before any broader promotion; document curriculum and model provenance.
2. **Agent engineering:** start with the debugger; integrate scoped tools, reproducible sandboxes, rollback, and the evidence schema. Keep Teacher reviews distinct from independent outcome verification.
3. **Evaluation:** own sealed tasks and customer acceptance rubrics; monitor contamination, inter-rater agreement, energy coverage and prospective validity.
4. **Commercial operations:** interview design partners, test the workbench, task and cell prices in euros, and replace annual revenue targets with monthly signed-contract and collection data.
5. **Physical engineering:** partner-owned guarded cell only; hazard analysis and supervised observation precede bounded autonomy.
6. **Pulse research:** compare ordinary euro billing with PLS compute settlement and PAR field observation on controlled pilots. No live token claim until technical, privacy and legal gates pass.

## Monthly review packet

Publish a dated internal packet containing: paid organisations and churn; accepted and attempted tasks by role; independent-verifier coverage; review minutes, elapsed time, GPU-board Wh and whole-system kWh per accepted task; direct cost and gross contribution; critical incidents and near misses; forecast-versus-actual variance; open rollback and privacy issues. Keep customer material private and publish only aggregate claims with denominators and methods.

The first packet cannot be filled from benchmark scores. It waits for prospective work records. The scorecard's evidence hash and the audit trail should be stored with each packet so later revisions can be traced.
